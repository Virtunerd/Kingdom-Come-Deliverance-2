# Silent Takedown

[Silent takedown](https://github.com/Virtunerd/Kingdom-Come-Deliverance-2/releases/download/kcd2/silenttakedown.zip/):
Knockouts and stealth kills are silent: sleeping NPCs do not wake up, nobody nearby hears anything or raises the alarm. Anyone who actually sees it still notices.

It works in any situation - like two NPCs are walking and you stealth kill the NPC behind the first one.
