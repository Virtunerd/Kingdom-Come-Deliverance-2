# Horsedeed

Buy it from its owner, steal it and make it legally yours on the spot, keep every horse you own in your own stable, and sell the ones you no longer need. No more trips to the black-market horse dealer.

## Two new options on every horse

Look at a horse that isn’t yours:

- **Tap E – Steal:** the normal theft. The horse is not yours and does not join your stable.
- **Hold E – Steal and transfer:** you steal the horse, and once you’re in the saddle Henry is entered as its keeper. It becomes your horse right away.

Witnesses still see a theft either way, so pick your moment.

## Buying a horse from its owner

Talk to the person a horse belongs to and choose [Buy horse]. They tell you the price, then you pick [Pay] or [Too expensive]. Buying is legal, no crime involved.

## Unique and named horses

Famous horses like Capon’s horse, Žižka’s horse, Hector or Medusa can now be bought and stolen too. You ride the actual horse you picked. As soon as you’re out of sight (30 m away, during a cutscene, after a scene change or a load), it becomes your own copy named “&lt;Name&gt; Skalitz”, for example “Attila Skalitz”. The original goes back to its owner, so quests and characters that need their horse keep working. Cart horses, battle horses and horses a quest is using right now stay off limits.

## Your stable (right Shift + F11)

A window that lists every horse you own, up to 64, spread over clickable pages:

- See each horse’s name, value and whether it’s your active horse, nearby or far away.
- **Make active:** switch to any of your horses, then call it as usual.
- **Sell:** sell a horse right from the window. Click once to see the price, click again to confirm.

The stable only works on foot, since the game opens no windows while you’re riding. Close it with right Shift + F11 or Esc.

## Selling at the black market

Both black-market horse dealers (at the Nomad camp near Trosky and near Kuttenberg) now buy horses you own. Choose [Sell my horse], answer “Are you sure?” with yes, done. Stolen horses still sell through the dealer’s own dialogue as before.

## Your gear goes with you

- When you switch, buy or transfer a horse, your saddle, bridle, caparison, horseshoes and saddlebag contents move to the new horse and are put on.
- A horse you buy comes without its seller’s gear. The seller keeps it.
- A horse you steal keeps its own gear in its saddlebags.
- When you sell a horse, everything it wears or carries goes into your inventory.

## Prices at a glance

| Action | Cost / payout |
|---|---|
| Steal | Free (it’s a crime) |
| Steal and transfer | 25 % of the horse’s value as keeper’s fee |
| Buy from the owner | 100 % of the horse’s value |
| Sell (stable or black market) | You receive the horse’s value + 10 % |

The horse’s value is the same one the game shows when you inspect a horse. If you can’t afford the keeper’s fee, only the normal steal is offered. You get the game’s usual “groschen received” notice after a sale.

## Settings

Everything can be changed in mod.cfg in the mod folder:

- **FeePercent:** keeper’s fee in %
- **BuyPercent:** purchase price in %; 0 turns buying off
- **SellBonusPercent:** extra on top of the value when selling
- **SellRequiresDealer:** 1 = selling only next to a black-market dealer
- **UnlockUnique:** 0 = unique horses stay locked
- **CarryGear / CarryBags:** gear and saddlebags move with you
- **SellerKeepsGear:** 0 = a bought horse keeps the seller’s gear

## Console

`hd_reclaim` adds the horse you’re standing next to (within 4 m) to your stable. It’s meant for a horse you owned before installing the mod that has wandered back to its old place.

## Compatibility

- Uses right Shift + F11. F11 on its own stays free for other mods.
- The stable lives in your save game.
- Languages: English and German.
