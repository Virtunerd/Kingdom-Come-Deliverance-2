# Silvertongue

[Silvertongue - Heartbroken to death](https://github.com/Virtunerd/Kingdom-Come-Deliverance-2/releases/download/kcd2/Silvertongue.zip/)

**Anyone can kill with a sword, a bow or poison. You only need one sentence.**

Henry walks up to a stranger wearing the friendliest face in all of Bohemia: “Can we talk for a moment?” A pause, a look, a familiar tone. Then he says it: *Your brother drowned. Your husband was hanged. They were all murdered. There was a fire, and the house was full of bodies.*

None of it is true, and it no longer matters.

You watch the words land. Their face crumbles and their voice breaks. Then they walk away in complete silence, to their bench. They sit down and weep. They call out for someone who never died: “Why?” You stand a few steps away and watch.

Then they clutch their chest, lie down in the grass, and never get up again.

## Why Silvertongue is so deliciously evil

- **The perfect murder without a murder.** No weapon, no blood on your hands, no witness who saw anything suspicious. No guard comes after you and no bounty is put on your head. Officially, they died of a broken heart.
- **In Henry’s own voice.** He lies with his real, fully voiced lines. This isn’t some stranger. It’s the lad from Skalitz that everyone trusts.
- **Grief that feels real.** Your victims mourn exactly like the game’s own bereaved: their own bench, tears, lamenting cries. It is uncomfortably real.
- **The mighty survive, but they don’t walk away untouched.** The bailiff’s son, the men of rank and name: they don’t die, they collapse unconscious. You can’t kill them, but you can bring them to their knees.
- **Guards are only human.** The man with the halberd who guarded the road yesterday is sitting on a bench in tears today.
- **Empty a whole village with nothing but words.** One by one. Everyone believes you, because who would ever make up something like that?

> “That was your tongue, not your arm.”

Play the saint who comforts widows, or the devil who makes them widows in the first place. Nobody in Kuttenberg knows who you really are. Good.

## Features

- **[Manipulate]:** a dialogue option on every open-world NPC. Quest characters are excluded.
- **Lies:** four dramatic lies, all spoken in Henry’s original voice.
- **Reactions:** NPCs answer with fitting vanilla voice lines and grieving facial expressions.
- **Mourning:** they mourn on the nearest bench, their own home bench first, or on the ground nearby.
- **Outcome:** ordinary NPCs die of grief. Important and immortal characters pass out and wake up later.
- **Daily routines:** schedules are suspended while an NPC mourns, so guards don’t wander off to change clothes.
- **No crime:** Henry is never punished.
