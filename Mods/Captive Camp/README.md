# Captive Camp

**[Screenshots here](images/)**

**A mod for Kingdom Come: Deliverance II by Virtunerd, version 1.0.0**

Run your own prison camp in medieval Bohemia. Pitch a ring of palisades anywhere in the open, take captives, break them at the pillory, put them to work and sell them to the buyers who come to your auction stage. When you move on, pack the camp up and pitch it again somewhere else.

---

## At a glance

- A complete camp that you can pitch anywhere in the open and pack up again.
- Staff in Sigismund's colours who guard the camp, keep the books and carry out your orders.
- Captives you take yourself or have your men bring in with the cage cart.
- Pillories and cages to break them, a tent to keep them, orders to give them.
- An auction on the stage with three kinds of buyers, outfits for the goods and delivery by wagon.
- Three upgrades: a work camp, a fighting ground and your own quarters with a bath and a bath maid.
- Optional quick orders through a radial menu.
- Nothing the camp spawns ends up in your save game.

---

## Features

### The camp

When you pitch the camp you get:

- a palisade ring with a gate,
- a pen for new captives,
- the captives' tent,
- two pillories for men and two cages for women,
- an auction stage,
- a pit,
- a kitchen, a wash tub and the steward's table where the injured are patched up,
- the camp chest,
- a covered wagon for deliveries.

Pack the camp up and pitch it again elsewhere whenever you like. Nothing the camp spawns is written to your save: after every load the camp is rebuilt from its anchor.

### The staff

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Overseer** | Watches the captives. Brings them to the pillories and cages and takes the broken ones out, sends the injured to be patched up, hands out punishments, and sends men out for fresh captives. |
| **Scribe** | Keeps the camp ledger. Calls the buyers for an auction and hands over the money they paid. |
| **Camp steward** | Keeps the stores. Builds the upgrades and packs up the camp. |
| **Wagon driver** | Drives the covered wagon and delivers what was sold. |
| **Camp guards** | Walk the palisade by day, keep watch by night, and eat at the camp tables when off duty. |
| **Gate guards** | Keep the gate. |
| **Pit guard** | Watches the pit. |
| **Foreman** | Runs the work camp and sells what you leave in the sales chest (work camp upgrade). |
| **Arena Warden** | Runs fights and executions on the fighting ground (fighting ground upgrade). |
| **Barkeeper** | Serves food and drink in your quarters and whistles for your dog (quarters upgrade). |

Ask any of them who they are.

### Taking captives

- Knock someone out and carry them into the pen. Put them down inside: a moment later the camp ties them up on its own, and the scribe enters them in the camp ledger.
- The ledger holds up to 16 captives.
- Quest characters and other well-known people are refused.

### Raids

Ask the overseer to send men out for fresh captives. Two of your guards take the cage cart:

| Destination | Away for |
|---|---|
| The nearest village | half a day |
| Kuttenberg | a day and a night |
| Troskowitz | a day and a night |

They come back with fresh captives for the pen. Ask the overseer where they are, and he tells you roughly when to expect them, for example "tomorrow morning" or "late this evening". While the pen is full, your men stay in the camp.

### Breaking captives

- The overseer brings captives from the pen: men to the pillories, women to the cages. You choose one, two, three, or as many as there are free places.
- After a full day they are broken and move into the captives' tent, where they sit, sleep and wait for orders.
- The overseer takes broken captives out of the pillory or the cage when you tell him.

### Orders for captives

Talk to a captive of the camp and choose **Orders**:

- wash at the tub,
- go and get patched up at the steward's table,
- back to the tent (ends whatever task they have),
- for women: become your bath maid (needs your own quarters).

### Punishments

- **Kitchen duty:** a day and a night at the cooking fire.
- **The pit:** use the shovel at the pit to choose who goes down. Nobody comes back from there.
- **Your iron cage:** lock a captive in the cage in your own quarters.

### The auction

1. Ask the scribe to put a captive before the stage. The buyers are called: a merchant from Kuttenberg, a knight's steward and a mine owner, each with a bodyguard and servants.
2. Present up to three captives at once. Pick an outfit for each of them: the clothes change what the bidders are willing to pay. You pay for the clothes when the bidding starts, and you get the money back if the auction is called off.
3. The buyers bid against each other, captive by captive. The highest bidder signs at the scribe, and the money goes into the camp chest. Ask the scribe for it.
4. The wagon driver delivers what was sold. You can ride along with him (six hours pass), let him drive alone, or skip the delivery and sell right away.

### Upgrades

Buy them from the camp steward:

| Upgrade | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Work camp** | 750 groschen | Workstations at the cooking fire, smokehouse, drying rack, grindstone, forge and herb bed, a wash tub, a stall and a gate of its own. The yield goes into chests, and the foreman sells whatever you leave in the sales chest. |
| **Fighting ground** | 500 groschen | A ring with a red and a blue corner, a palisade and a gate of its own. Tell the Arena Warden who fights and whether with weapons (they get cheap armour) or with their fists, or have him carry out an execution. |
| **Your own quarters** | 1000 groschen | A hut with a bed outside the ring, a bar, a smokehouse, a bath and an iron cage. Nobody else gets through that gate. |

In your quarters:

- **The bar:** beer (2 groschen), a cup of wine (3), mead (4), bread (6) and salami (6). You pay like anyone else. The barkeeper also whistles for your dog.
- **The bath:** choose a female captive as your bath maid. She lives in your quarters. Take a bath, she comes to the tub, two hours pass, and you come out washed, scrubbed and patched up.
- **The iron cage:** for the captive of your choice.

### Quick orders (optional)

With **Virtunerd Radial** or **Area Hit Vials** installed, hold **Ctrl** and open the **Camp** tab:

- pitch or pack up the camp,
- fill the pillories and cages,
- send all the injured to be patched up,
- start an auction,
- give orders to the captive you are looking at: into the pillory or the cage, into your iron cage, to the kitchen, to the pit, to the auction, back to the tent.

Without a radial menu only these quick orders are missing. The staff's dialogues do everything else.

## Getting started

1. Find open, fairly level ground away from towns.
2. Pitch the camp: hold Ctrl and use the Camp tab, or open the console and type `captive_camp_pitch`.
3. Knock someone out, carry them into the pen and put them down. A moment later the camp ties them up.
4. Talk to the overseer about the pillories and the cages.
5. Talk to the scribe about the auction and to the camp steward about upgrades.
6. To move on, ask the camp steward to pack up the camp.

## Console commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `captive_camp_pitch` | Pitches the camp near you. |
| `captive_camp_strike` | Packs up the camp. |

## Tips

- Only unconscious people can be taken. Carry them in and put them down inside the pen.
- Your raiders stay in the camp while the pen is full, so make room first.
- You pay for the stage clothes only when the bidding starts.
- Riding along with the wagon driver makes six hours pass.

## Compatibility

- No other mods are required. Works with new and existing saves.
- Captive Camp ships its own copy of the NPC switch tree (`AI/npc/basic/switch/switch.xml`): vanilla plus one small hook. Other mods that replace this file can conflict. Load Captive Camp after them.
- A busy camp has a few dozen people in it. On weaker PCs this costs frame rate, especially during fights.
- Text is available in English and German.

## Known limitations

- Captives walk with free hands when the overseer leads them. The game has no walking animation with tied hands.
- The ledger holds up to 16 captives.

## Uninstalling

Pack up the camp first, then remove the mod.

## Content note

Captivity, forced labour, a slave auction and executions, told in the setting of the game.
