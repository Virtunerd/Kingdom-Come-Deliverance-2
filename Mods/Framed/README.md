# Framed

**Download:** [Framed.zip](https://github.com/Virtunerd/Kingdom-Come-Deliverance-2/releases/download/kcd2/Framed.zip)

# Plant goods on people, report them to the guards and watch the law take its course. Every step uses vanilla animations, vanilla AI and the real dungeons of the world.

## How it works

- **Plant:** start pickpocketing someone and press **U** during the minigame. The pickpocketing ends successfully, your victim stands still and an empty container opens. Whatever you put into it ends up in their pockets.
- **Report:** talk to any guard and choose *"I want to report a thief."*
- **Arrest:** the guard runs to the person, who waits nervously and surrenders when the guard arrives (men kneel, women cower). The guard seizes the planted goods, binds the thief and leads them away, waiting if they fall behind.
- **Dungeon:** once out of your sight, the thief ends up in the dungeon of the map and sits there in shackles until you clear their name.

## Clear their name
Changed your mind? There are three ways to set a prisoner free:

- Tell any guard *"I was wrong about the thief I reported."*
- Talk to the prisoner at their cell: *"You are free. I was wrong about you."*
- Use the cell door: **Release prisoner**.

Released prisoners return to the place where they were arrested.

## Dungeons

- Trosky region: the cell in Trosky castle.
- Kuttenberg region: the three cells of the town hall dungeon.
- On maps without a dungeon the guard only seizes the goods and lets the thief go.

## Good to know

- Prisoners are kept in your save and stay in their cell until you release them.
- Planted goods and a report in progress are not kept in a save.
- Some background extras may not stop when reported. If the guard cannot reach the thief within 3 minutes, the report ends with "The reported thief got away."
- Your victim stands still while the container is open, just like during the minigame.

## Compatibility
Framed adds a function to *Libs/UI/Pickpocketing.gfx* and a hook to *AI/npc/basic/switch/switch.xml*. Both files already contain the changes of my mods Fingers, Offering, Shackles and Shouts, so those keep working. If you use any of them, load Framed after them. Other mods that replace these two files are not compatible.
Optional: with Shackles installed, prisoners wear iron shackles instead of rope.

## Languages
English, German.
