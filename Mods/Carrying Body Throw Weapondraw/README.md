# Reflex Throw

Carrying a body and suddenly need your hands free? Henry no longer has to kneel down and gently lay it on the ground. The body leaves his shoulder at once - thrown far, or simply dropped when you go for your weapon.

## Features

- **Throw** - tap **E** while carrying a body (dead or unconscious) and Henry throws it far in the direction you are looking. No put-down animation: the body flies straight from his shoulder as a ragdoll.
- **Drop & draw** - press **1-4** or **B** (gamepad: **X**) while carrying: the body drops straight from the shoulder and the key then does exactly what it does in vanilla - 1-4 take the weapon from that quick slot, B draws/sheathes. Your quick slots are never changed.
- **Vanilla stays** - hold **E** and Henry puts the body down as usual.
- A **"Throw"** button hint appears next to the vanilla "Put down" as soon as the body is on Henry's shoulder.
- Picking up a body with a drawn weapon works as in vanilla but with active slot ready to fight after dropping the body.

## Controls (while carrying a body)

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Tap E | Throw the body |
| 1-4 | Drop the body, take the weapon from that quick slot |

## Installation

- Requires an **ASI loader** (e.g. Ultimate ASI Loader as dinput8.dll in *KingdomComeDeliverance2\Bin\Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO*).
- Copy **ReflexThrow.asi** from the mod folder into *KingdomComeDeliverance2\Bin\Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO*.
- The instant release from the shoulder and the weapon keys are done natively - without the ASI the mod does nothing and everything stays vanilla.

## Compatibility

- Replaces *Libs/Config/defaultActionHelp.xml* (button hints). Other mods replacing this file can hide the "Throw" hint - throwing itself still works.
- Vanilla put-down and other mods that put bodies down are not touched; only Reflex Throw's own keys skip the animation.
