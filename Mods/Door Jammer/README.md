# Door Jammer

# Jam a door shut and keep everyone out. Or in.

Walk up to any closed, unlocked door and press **V** to wedge it. A jammed door stays shut for every NPC: owners, guards, thieves and quest walkers can't open it, unlock it, lockpick it or slip through it. Press **V** at the door again to pull the wedge out.

## Features

- **Real barrier:** a jammed door blocks NPCs like a wall. They walk up, try the door and stay outside.
- **No sneaking past:** NPCs that the game would normally teleport through a blocked door get put back on their side.
- **Both sides:** jam or unjam from inside or outside.
- **Saved:** jammed doors stay jammed after saving and loading.
- **Safe for vanilla doors:** doors that are already locked are left alone.
- English and German text.

## How to use

- Close the door.
- Look at it and press **V** – "Jam door".
- Press **V** again – "Remove wedge".

## Known limitations

- **Skipping time** (waiting, sleeping, fast travel) can still move NPCs past a jammed door. The game relocates NPCs during a skip without using doors, so a mod can't reliably stop it.
- The game may occasionally spawn a resident directly inside a jammed room.

## Compatibility

- No vanilla files are replaced, so it works alongside mods that change doors or lockpicking.

## Uninstalling
**Remove all wedges before uninstalling.** Otherwise those doors stay locked and can't be lockpicked in that save.
