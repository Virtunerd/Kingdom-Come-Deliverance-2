[Alle Versionen ansehen und herunterladen](https://github.com/Virtunerd/Kingdom-Come-Deliverance-2/tree/main/mods/houseofbardi)

[House of Bardi - Bank Account](https://github.com/Virtunerd/Kingdom-Come-Deliverance-2/releases/download/kcd2/houseofbardi.zip/) 

Florentine bankers who broke with the Church to serve the people. Lorenzo Bardi stands outside the Italian Court in Kuttenberg, his nephew Niccolo in Troskowitz.  They buy gold, silver, chalices, crosses and jewellery, and keep your groschen. Open an account, get a bank letter, deposit and withdraw at either table. Both bankers are marked on the map. Settings in mod.cfg.
